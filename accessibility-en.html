Accessibility statement

Last updated: October 20, 2026
This is a translation. If there is any conflict, the Hebrew version prevails.

We want NextRep to be easy for everyone to use, including people with disabilities. The app was developed in accordance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1) as far as possible.

What we have done

  • Full support for Hebrew and for right-to-left text direction.
  • High color contrast and a dark mode that adapts to your phone settings.
  • Large buttons that are easy to tap, and text descriptions for buttons shown as icons.
  • Respect for the device's "Reduce motion" setting: animations are disabled when the setting is on.
  • Support for text enlargement and for the device's screen readers.

Known limitations

  • Some charts present visual information. The key data also appears as text.
  • Exercise demonstrations are images, and their description appears in writing next to them.

Ran into a problem?

If something in the app is not accessible to you, we would be glad to hear about it and fix it. Write to us: ofek4business@gmail.com, or via "Contact us" in the settings. We will try to reply within 7 business days.
