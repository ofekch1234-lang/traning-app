Support

We're here for any question about NextRep
Email us: support@nextrep.co.il
Or from the app: Profile → Help & contact. We usually reply within one business day.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

iPhone subscriptions are managed by Apple: open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → NextRep, or in the app go to the subscription screen → Manage subscription. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid period.

I switched phones and my subscription is missing

Sign in to the same account and tap "Restore purchases" on the subscription screen.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Help & contact → Delete account and data. All your data is permanently deleted. Deleting the account does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel it separately.

My friend didn't get the invite reward

If it wasn't detected automatically, your friend can type your invite code in the welcome questionnaire. If that doesn't help, email us with the code and we'll fix it.
