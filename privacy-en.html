Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026
This is a translation. If there is any conflict, the Hebrew version prevails.
In short: we keep only what is needed for the app to work, we do not sell data and we do not show ads. Your progress photos are private. You can ask to receive your data or delete it at any time.

1. Who is responsible for the data

Ofek BaDigital, the operator of NextRep. For privacy inquiries: support@nextrep.co.il.

2. What data we collect

  • Account details: name, email and password. The password is stored encrypted and we cannot see it.
  • Phone: if you provided it to identify a payment.
  • Workout data: plans, workouts, sets, weights and times.
  • Health information: body weight, goals, meals and nutritional values, and progress photos. This is sensitive information, and we treat it accordingly.
  • Group content: messages, reactions and challenges.
  • Payments: amount, plan, date, and name and phone as entered on the payment page. Credit card details are stored only by the payment processor.
  • Technical information: a device identifier for notifications (Push), login times and a log of AI usage (number and cost, without content).
  • Support requests: the content of your message and your contact details.

3. Why we use the data

  • To operate the Service: to save and display your workouts, meals and progress, and to sync between devices.
  • To send notifications you allowed.
  • To manage the free plan, Premium, invite perks and payments.
  • To provide support, improve the Service and prevent misuse.

We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising and do not build marketing profiles from it.

4. Who we share data with

Only with providers needed to operate the Service, and each only with what it needs:

  • Supabase: hosting of the database, login and photos, on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt).
  • Vercel: hosting of the app website.
  • Anthropic: AI analysis of meals. Only the text or photo you chose to analyze is sent, without your name or account details. Under Anthropic's commercial terms of use, by default the data is not used to train their models.
  • Grow (Meshulam): payment processing.
  • Open Food Facts: when you scan a barcode, only the barcode number is sent.
  • Group members: only the information described in the Terms (messages, workout summaries and records).

Data may also be disclosed if the law or a competent authority requires it.

5. Security

Access to data is restricted at the server level, so each user sees only their own data. Communication is encrypted (HTTPS), and progress photos are kept in private storage. No security is perfect, and if we discover a security incident that affects you, we will notify you as the law requires.

6. Data retention

We keep the data as long as the account is active. After a deletion request, we will delete the account and the data within 30 days, except for payment records that we are required to keep by law.

7. Your rights

  • To review your data and receive a copy of it.
  • To ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • To ask us to delete your account and all your data, via "Delete account" in the settings or by email.
  • To turn off notifications at any time in the app settings.

8. Minors

The Service is intended for users aged 16 and over. If we learn that data was collected from someone under this age, we will delete it.

9. Local storage (Cookies)

The app stores technical data on your phone, such as login, an active workout and preferences, so it can work even without reception. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking.

10. Changes to this policy

We will notify you in the app of any material change. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.
